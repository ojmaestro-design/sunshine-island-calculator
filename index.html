How runes work

In the game, a rune boosts all your fields, plantations or mines for 48 hours.

When a rune is on in your game, tap the same rune at the top of the calculator. It glows, shows when you switched it on, and every total includes its boost.

To switch one off, tap it and answer Yes.

Add to your home screen

iPhone or iPad: open this page in Safari or Chrome, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.

Easiest: on that screen, turn Open as Web App off. The icon then opens the page in your browser, with all your levels and buildings already there.

Android: open it in Chrome, tap the three dots, then Add to Home screen.

Important: the home-screen icon keeps its own copy of your setup, separate from your browser. Levels and buildings you set in one do not appear in the other by themselves.

To move them across: in the one you have set up, go to My buildings, Save and press Copy my setup. Then open the other one, go to the same place, press Paste a setup, paste, and press Load this text.

If that feels like too much, just keep using the page in your browser and bookmark it.
